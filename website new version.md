# CREATIVE MANIFESTO

## The Website Is Not A Landing Page

Antromat is not presented as software.

Antromat is presented as a publication introducing a new way of running businesses.

The visitor is reading Issue 01 of Antromat Journal.

Every section feels like another editorial article.

Every image feels curated.

Every sentence earns its place.

Nothing exists only for decoration.

## The Core Philosophy

> Antromat reveals what matters.

Businesses already create thousands of conversations.

Messages.

Invoices.

Orders.

Payments.

Voice notes.

Photos.

Documents.

Most businesses already have information.

They don't have clarity.

Antromat creates clarity.

The website should visually demonstrate clarity.

## The Design Promise

The website should feel:

* Calm enough to read.

* Beautiful enough to remember.

* Clear enough to understand.

* Premium enough to trust.

## Design Mission Statement

Build the most tasteful enterprise AI website possible.

It should not resemble a startup template.

It should resemble a coffee-table magazine introducing a new category of software.

# BRAND PHILOSOPHY

## What Antromat Believes

Businesses should never depend on memory.

Knowledge should belong to the company.

Operations should become visible.

AI should disappear into the workflow.

Software should reduce noise instead of adding it.

## What Antromat Is

An AI Chief of Staff.

A business memory system.

A decision layer over everyday business activity.

A calm operating system for business intelligence.

## What Antromat Is Not

* CRM replacement.

* WhatsApp bot.

* AI chatbot.

* Automation platform.

* AI agent marketplace.

* Productivity tool.

Those may exist inside the product.

They are not the identity.

## Brand Sentence

> Every business generates knowledge. Antromat remembers it.

# EDITORIAL GRID

## Layout Philosophy

Everything follows a 12-column editorial grid.

Whitespace creates hierarchy.

Image right.

Article B

Image left.

Headline right.

Article D

Centered quote.

Repeat rhythm.

## Content Width

Headlines never exceed eight columns.

Body copy never exceeds six columns.

Captions never exceed three columns.

## Full Bleed Moments

Only chapter openings.

Only photography.

No UI screenshots full bleed except hero.

# SPACING SYSTEM

## Vertical Rhythm

Between major articles:

160–200px

Between heading and paragraph:

32px

Between paragraph and image:

72px

Between image and caption:

20px

Between caption and next content:

48px

## Horizontal Rhythm

Large margins.

No edge-to-edge content except photography.

Content breathes.

## Negative Space Rules

Empty space is intentional.

Never fill empty space with icons.

Never fill empty space with patterns.

Never fill empty space with decorative text.

High contrast text.

Readable body copy.

Motion respects reduced-motion preference.

# IMPLEMENTATION RULES

Do not use gradients.

Do not use glassmorphism.

Do not use neon colors.

Do not use decorative AI illustrations.

Do not use more than one focus color per viewport.

Do not overcrowd layouts.

Do not exceed four lines of body copy before introducing whitespace.

Every viewport must answer exactly one question.

Every image must have a caption.

Every major section begins with an article number.

Every major transition feels like turning a magazine page.
