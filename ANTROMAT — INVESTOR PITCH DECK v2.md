# SLIDE 2 — THE PROBLEM

## Businesses don't have a data problem. They have a memory problem.

Every day, businesses create hundreds of operational events.

- Customer enquiries arrive on WhatsApp and phone calls.
- Quotations are shared as PDFs.
- Workers send production updates through photos and voice notes.
- Dispatch updates live in chat groups.
- Payments are tracked in spreadsheets and notebooks.

The information already exists.

**The context disappears.**

### What this looks like today

- Leads are forgotten.
- Follow-ups are missed.
- Owners call employees for status updates.
- Payment reminders happen manually.
- Knowledge lives inside people's heads.

**The owner becomes the company's memory.**

That does not scale.
