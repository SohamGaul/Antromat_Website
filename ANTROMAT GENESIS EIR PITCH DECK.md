## DESIGN SYSTEM

Theme:
Industrial Operating System.
Swiss editorial meets enterprise control room.

Colors:
- Background: warm off-white (#F7F5F2)
- Black panels.
- Yellow highlight (#F5C400).
- Red only for revenue leakage alerts.
- Thin technical borders and labels.
- Lots of whitespace.

Typography:
Large bold uppercase headlines.
Small monospace section labels.
Minimal body text.

Do NOT use:
- Gradients.
- Startup illustrations.
- AI brain graphics.
- Stock business people.
- Blue SaaS templates.

The presentation should feel like Apple's Human Interface Manual crossed with a factory operations dashboard.

Every slide uses SECTION XX / 16 labels.
