CORE MISSION

"Work should not wait for people." Antromat eliminates operational latency by converting daily, unstructured business conversations (WhatsApp audio, Hinglish/Marathi text, missed calls, handwritten slips) into automated, deterministic enterprise execution across CRM, quotation generation, GST billing, and payment follow-ups.

01

The Ground Reality & Operational Chaos

94% of Indian MSMEs are digitally connected on chat, but operationally fragmented:

WhatsApp & Audio: Inquiries arrive as Hinglish voice notes, photos & chats with zero CRM entry.
Phone Calls: Rate negotiations & site instructions locked in memory; zero audit trail.
Paper Ledgers: Loose dispatch slips and handwritten estimates pile up on counters.
Spreadsheets: Excel updated days late with broken formulas & forgotten receivables.
Key Insight: MSMEs will never adopt complex desktop ERPs. Automation must sit directly on top of existing communication channels.

03

4-Tier System Architecture

1. INGEST
WhatsApp Cloud API, Voice Notes, Email Webhooks, Paper Challan OCR.
2. AI NLP CORE
Hinglish/Marathi entity extraction (Customer, SKU, Qty, Rate, Location) with >99% confidence.
3. ORCHESTRATE
Deterministic state machine, PDF quotation engine, scheduled payment reminders.
4. CONNECT
Tally Prime / ERP, GST Invoicing, Google Calendar, Owner Operations Cockpit.
